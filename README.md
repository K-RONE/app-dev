# Harry Potter

## My Favorite Series {#favorite-series}

### Overview

**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series by *J.K. Rowling* about a boy who discovers he is a wizard and attends Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. It has 7 books and 8 movies. :zap:

> Friendship, courage, and choosing what is right over what is easy are the heart of the story.

---
